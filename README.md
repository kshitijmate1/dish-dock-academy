# DISH Dock Academy - LPN Edition

Quick flashcard quiz for the DISH warehouse team on ShipHero Mobile Receiving with LPNs and how LPNs affect putaway, replen, picking and cycle counts.

- 50 Right/Wrong cards: 20 critical (2 pts), 20 necessary (1 pt), 10 basic (1 pt) = 70 pts
- Pass mark: 47 / 70
- Study mode, shuffled quiz, review of misses, printable certificate

Single static file: `index.html`. Training aid only - the DISH SOP and your lead have the final word.
